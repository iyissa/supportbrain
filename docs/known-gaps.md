# FinFlow KB v1: known coverage gaps

Content that a customer could reasonably need but that doesn't exist in v1. Log any Act 1 question that fails because of one of these as a coverage gap, not a retrieval failure.

| Gap | Where customers hit it | Why it's out of v1 |
|---|---|---|
| Restrictions that need transaction information | Suspension article, "follow the steps on your account banner" | Fixable-restrictions article cut from v1 |
| Restrictions that need a device check | Suspension article, "follow the steps on your account banner" | Fixable-restrictions article cut from v1 |
| Restrictions that need dispute evidence | Suspension article, "follow the steps on your account banner" | Fixable-restrictions article cut from v1 |
| How to cancel a paid plan | Refund timelines article, which states it's out of scope | No cancellation article in v1 |
| Merchant-purchase refunds | Refund timelines article, which points customers to the merchant | R1 scopes v1 to plan refunds |

Identity and document restrictions (verification failed, information incomplete, expired documents) are covered by the KYC article under A18.
