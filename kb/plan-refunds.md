## Do I get a refund when I cancel my FinFlow paid plan?

Yes. When you cancel a FinFlow paid plan or subscription, you get money back for the days you paid for but won't use. You may also see this called getting your money back, a cancellation refund, or a reimbursement. This article doesn't cover how to cancel a plan or refunds from shops and merchants. For a refund from a merchant, contact the merchant first, because the merchant decides whether and when to send it.

## How much will I get back when I cancel my plan?

When you cancel your FinFlow plan, you get back the cost of the unused days in your billing period, counted from the date you cancel. There's no cancellation fee, so the refund is the full value of the days you won't use. For example, if you cancel with 10 days left in your billing period, you get back the cost of those 10 days.

## How long does a plan refund take, and where does it go?

A plan refund takes 3-5 business days after your cancellation is confirmed. The refund always goes to your FinFlow balance, not to a card or another bank account. The refund is calculated and sent automatically, so you don't need to request it or contact anyone, and you'll get a notification when it arrives. If the refund hasn't reached your balance within 7 business days of your cancellation being confirmed, email the support team with your plan name, your cancellation date, and your account number.

## What happens to my plan refund if my account is restricted?

If your account is restricted, your plan refund still arrives in your balance, but it stays frozen with the rest of your balance until the restriction is lifted. A restricted account means you can log in and see your balance, but you can't make transactions. Your refund is safe and becomes available as soon as the restriction ends. To find out why your account is restricted and how the restriction ends, read [Restricted account: what happens when FinFlow reviews your case]. It explains what a restriction means, what you can do in the meantime, and how long a review takes.
