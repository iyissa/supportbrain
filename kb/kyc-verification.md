## What can I do before my identity is verified?

Before you complete Tier 1 identity verification, you can log in and view your account, but you can't send payments. You may also see identity verification called KYC, an identity check, or account verification. Sending payments unlocks as soon as your Tier 1 verification is approved. Tier 1 needs one photo ID and a selfie, and it's usually checked automatically within 24 hours.

## What documents do I need to verify my identity?

To complete Tier 1 identity verification, you need one photo ID and a selfie. The accepted photo IDs are a passport, a national ID card, or a driver's licence. The selfie is a liveness check, which confirms you're a real person matching the ID. You can upload your ID as a JPG, PNG, or PDF file, up to 10 MB.

## How do I verify my identity?

There are 4 steps to verify your identity:

1. Choose the document you want to use.
2. Upload a photo or scan of the document.
3. Take a selfie for the liveness check.
4. Submit your verification.

You can start these steps in the app. If you can't log in, for example because you lost the phone you use for 2FA, start from the login screen by choosing "Can't use your codes?" instead.

You'll be notified when your verification is approved or rejected.

## What are verification tiers and what does each one need?

FinFlow has 3 verification tiers, and your daily transfer limit rises with each tier:

1. Tier 1 needs a photo ID and a selfie.
2. Tier 2 needs everything in Tier 1, plus proof of address: a utility bill or bank statement dated within the last 3 months.
3. Tier 3 needs everything in Tier 2, plus proof of your source of funds: a payslip or bank statement showing where your money comes from.

The daily limits are $500 for Tier 1, $2,000 for Tier 2, and $10,000 for Tier 3. If a payment fails because it would go over your limit, completing the next tier raises your limit.

## How long does identity verification take?

How long identity verification takes depends on your tier. Tier 1 is usually checked automatically, which takes up to 24 hours. If an automatic check takes longer than 24 hours, it moves to manual review, which takes 1-3 business days. Tier 2 and Tier 3 usually go straight to manual review, which takes 1-3 business days. You'll be notified when your verification is approved or rejected. If you haven't heard back within 4 business days of submitting your documents, email the support team with your account number and the date you submitted your documents.

## Why was my identity verification rejected?

When your identity verification is rejected, FinFlow shows you the reason. The most common reasons are a blurry photo, details that don't match your account, or an expired document. You can fix the problem and submit your documents again. If your documents are rejected 3 times within 24 hours, your case goes to manual review instead, which takes 1-3 business days. You'll be notified of the result when the manual review is complete.

## What happens if my account is restricted because of my identity documents or information?

Your account can be restricted for 3 reasons linked to identity verification, and each one ends when you update what's missing. If your verification failed, submit your documents again. If your identity information is incomplete, add the missing information shown on your account banner. If your documents have expired, upload a current document. To submit documents, follow the 4 steps in "How do I verify my identity?" in this article: choose a document, upload it, take a selfie, and submit. After you submit, the restriction is lifted once your verification is approved, which takes up to 24 hours for an automatic check or 1-3 business days for a manual review. A restricted account means you can log in and see your balance, but you can't make transactions, and your money is safe until the restriction ends. To find out what else you can do during a restriction, read [Restricted account: what happens when FinFlow reviews your case]. It explains what a restriction means, what you can do in the meantime, and how long a review takes.
