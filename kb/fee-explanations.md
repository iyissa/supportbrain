## What does FinFlow charge fees for?

FinFlow charges fees on some payments but not others, and on paid plans. You may also see a fee described as a charge, a service charge, or money deducted from your balance. There are 4 types of payment, each with its own fee rule:

1. Transfers to another FinFlow account are free.
2. Transfers to a bank account carry a flat fee of $1.00.
3. International transfers carry a 1.5% fee, plus a 0.5% exchange-rate margin.
4. Card payments are free.

If you're on a paid plan, the plan is charged from your FinFlow balance at the start of each billing period, and the price is shown on the plan page.

## How do I know what fee I'll pay before I send money?

You see the fee on the confirmation screen before you send a payment. The confirmation screen shows the fee for that specific payment, so you can check it before you commit. If you don't want to pay the fee, you can cancel at the confirmation screen and nothing is charged.

## How is the exchange rate worked out on an international transfer?

On an international transfer, FinFlow converts your money at the market exchange rate plus a margin of 0.5%. The margin means you get slightly less of the other currency than the market rate alone would give you. The margin is separate from the 1.5% transfer fee, so an international transfer costs you both. For example, on a $100 international transfer, you pay a $1.50 fee, so $101.50 leaves your balance. The 0.5% margin means the recipient gets an amount worth about $0.50 less in the other currency than the market rate would give. The confirmation screen shows the fee and the final exchange rate before you send the transfer.

## Why was more money taken from my balance than I sent?

More money is taken from your balance than you sent because the fee is charged on top of the amount you send. The fee is deducted from your balance as a separate line item. For example, if you send a bank transfer, the recipient gets the full amount you entered, and the flat fee of $1.00 appears as its own line in your transaction history. Transfers to other FinFlow accounts and card payments are free, so the amount taken matches the amount sent.

## Do I get my fee back if a payment fails or is reversed?

Yes. If a payment fails or is reversed, the fee for that payment is refunded in full to your balance. If a payment completes, the fee isn't refunded, even if you later ask the recipient to return the money. The refunded fee returns to your balance together with the payment, within 2-3 business days.

## Do I pay fees while my account is restricted?

No. While your account is restricted, you don't pay transaction fees, because you can't make transactions. Your paid plan stays active, but plan fees are not charged during the restriction. Billing resumes when the restriction is lifted, and you aren't charged for the paused period. If a payment was in progress when your account was restricted, that payment is reversed and its fee is refunded to your balance, where both stay frozen until the restriction is lifted. A restricted account means you can log in and see your balance, but you can't make transactions. To find out why your account is restricted and how the restriction ends, read [Restricted account: what happens when FinFlow reviews your case]. It explains what a restriction means, what you can do in the meantime, and how long a review takes.
