## Why is my account restricted and what do I do?

FinFlow calls this state "restricted." You may also see it described as "suspended" or "banned." When your account is restricted, you can still log in and see your balance, but you can't make transactions.

If your account is restricted because of suspected fraud, a sanctions check, a legal or regulatory hold, or a terms-of-service issue, our support team reviews these cases and contacts you with next steps within 3-5 business days. That first contact tells you the next steps. It doesn't resolve the issue. How long resolution takes depends on the type of restriction.

If the support team hasn't contacted you within 5 business days, email us with your account number and the date your account was restricted.

If your reason isn't suspected fraud, a sanctions check, a legal or regulatory hold, or a terms-of-service issue, check your account banner for the steps that end the restriction. If the banner asks for identity documents or information, read [Identity verification: documents, tiers, and review times]. It explains which documents to upload, how to submit them, and how long review takes. For any other reason, follow the steps on your account banner. A full article on restrictions you can fix yourself is coming soon.

## What can I do while my account is restricted?

You can log in and view your balance, but you can't make any transactions. If your account banner asks you to submit documents or information, complete that as your first step toward lifting the restriction. If your account is restricted because of suspected fraud, a sanctions check, a legal or regulatory hold, or a terms-of-service issue, our support team reviews these cases and contacts you with next steps within 3-5 business days. Your money is safe and will be available once the restriction is lifted. If the support team hasn't contacted you within 5 business days, email us with your account number and the date your account was restricted.

## What happens to a payment that was in progress when my account was restricted?

If you had a payment in progress when the restriction was placed on your account, the payment is reversed back to your balance. The returned funds stay frozen with the rest of your balance until the restriction is lifted, and your money is safe in the meantime. The restriction is lifted once you complete the steps on your account banner or the support team finishes its review. A payment that had already completed before the restriction can't be reversed this way. You'll be notified when the reversed funds reach your balance. If you don't see your reversed payment in your balance within 2-3 business days, email the support team with the transaction details, your account number, and the date your account was restricted.

## What happens to my money if my restriction can't be lifted?

If your account is restricted because of a confirmed sanctions match or a legal or regulatory hold, your funds stay frozen and are handled as the relevant authority, such as a court or regulator, instructs. In these cases, FinFlow can't promise a date when your money will be released, because the decision isn't FinFlow's to make. If suspected fraud is confirmed, or a terms-of-service violation is serious, FinFlow may keep the restriction in place or close the account. The support team tells you which, and what happens to your balance, when it contacts you. A restricted account means you can log in and see your balance, but you can't make transactions. The support team contacts you with next steps within 3-5 business days of the restriction. If the support team hasn't contacted you within 5 business days, email us with your account number and the date your account was restricted.
